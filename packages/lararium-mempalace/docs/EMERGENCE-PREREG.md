## Preconditions — no bed result counts until these stand green

P1. **The store reads truly.** Per plane: the status verb's count equals a
    direct sqlite/collection count taken by a channel the verb does not own.
    (The form-collection mismatch and the structure page-cap both die first.)
P2. **Byte-identical double-run.** Every organ feeding a bed, run twice on the
    same input, emits identical bytes. Total orders at every ranking site.
P3. **Idempotent pass-2.** A second fan-out pass over an unchanged bed lands
    zero new records.
P4. **Chain inequality as a standing invariant.** On every bed,
    H(form) ≤ H(structure) ≤ H(content). A violation names a broken
    coarsening map — an instrument defect, never a finding.

## The criteria — what "emergence" means, priced in bits

C1. **The grammar beats ignorance, and meaning carries it.**
    On each bed: total code l(G) + l(D|G) beats the ignorance code, AND the
    savings on the real bed strictly exceed the savings on its seeded
    shuffled twin. Vow: real ≫ shuffled, or the "structure" belongs to the
    alphabet, not the text. Every bed run emits its null twin in the same
    invocation — no naked numbers.

C2. **Given-vs-induced address agreement** (enveloped corpuses only).
    The wrapper hands a given containment path (the lar: address); the corpus
    earns an induced one (the membership tree). Vow: on the bags bed the two
    trees agree above a permuted-address null (rank agreement over ultrametric
    distances). Divergence localizes to nameable records or the claim fails.

C3. **Boundary recall vs null — with the cardinality caveat.**
    Boundary reads (cosine-drop / novelty, per the grain's own specification)
    beat their circular-shift null. ABSOLUTE boundary counts stay unciteable
    until the cardinality under-count (every arm inferring 2–27 cuts where
    30–99 stand) carries its MDL cure. Ratios and recalls may speak; counts
    may not.

C4. **The coarsening stops paying somewhere.**
    Per bed, report the level past which further coarsening saves no bits
    (the minimal-sufficient-statistic stop). Vow: the stop exists and lands
    at a content-bearing level, not at the degenerate top. A bed whose stop
    sits at "one symbol for everything" reads as the silence attractor and
    fails the run.

C5. **The anti-apophenia re-grounding** (library bed).
    On coherent ground the machina refuses ghosts: no pattern-claim admits
    whose code fails to beat its null twin's. The old H¹ = 0 phrasing retires;
    the property re-states as C1 + C3 holding jointly at scale.

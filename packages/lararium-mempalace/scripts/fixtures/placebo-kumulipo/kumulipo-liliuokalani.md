# The Kumulipo — Liliʻuokalani Translation (1897)

Queen Liliʻuokalani's English translation of the Hawaiian creation chant, rendered while she was held under house arrest at ʻIolani Palace (1895) and completed at Washington, D.C., May 20, 1897. The koʻihonua (genealogical cosmogony) is attributed to the eighteenth-century chanter Keaulumoku and moves from the slime that established the earth, through sea and land life, to the genealogy of the ruling line.

Combined here from the per-era pages of the sacred-texts.com edition into one witness text, in reading order. Era headers and page markers `{p. N}` preserved.

# Mount-Point — Trait

<<~ ahu #contract >>

## Contract

A Trait seats a Power **in the practitioner**: engraved as memetic code, sung into implanted crystal, branded on the aura. Capacity is **7 + Thought** slots (canonical, Vastlands Guidebook). One Power per slot unless a feature says otherwise.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>

## Activation

A trait-seated Power activates from the self — range and target measured from the practitioner. No object to draw, lose, or have stolen.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #cost >>

## Cost

Pay Life equal to P (Ability Points at 1:1 parity). Double without the relevant skill. The `imbued` hook locks the Life until the Power deactivates.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #failure >>

## Failure

A failed activation runs the Danger Roll → Corruption procedure. Corruption on a trait writes back onto the practitioner's body/mind/aura — the host carries the scar.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #removal >>

## Removal

Traits resist casual removal. Re-engraving, corruption treatment, or a Path feature changes a trait. Overflow past 7 + Thought spills to Burden.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #overflow >>

## Overflow

Past capacity, additional patterns become Burdens (−1 to all rolls each) or wait for an Item/Site seat.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #aftermath >>

## Aftermath

- Thought is a living attribute; capacity moves with it. Confirm whether mid-session Thought loss can orphan a seated trait.

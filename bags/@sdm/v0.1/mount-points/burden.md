# Mount-Point — Burden

<<~ ahu #contract >>
## Contract

A Burden seats a Power **against the practitioner**: a curse, compulsion, affliction, or the overflow from a full Trait/Item. Capacity is **20 slots** (canonical, Vastlands Guidebook). Each occupied Burden slot imposes **−1 to all rolls**.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>
## Activation

A Burden often activates involuntarily — on a trigger, a condition, or the referee's call — rather than at the practitioner's choosing. The malign knowledge weighs whether or not it is used.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #cost >>
## Cost

The standing −1 per slot is the cost, paid continuously. Some Burdens also demand Life or a sacrifice when they fire.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #failure >>
## Failure

A Burden rarely "fails" — it afflicts. Botching its resistance deepens it: another slot, a worse trigger, or a Corruption tick.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #removal >>
## Removal

Remove by treatment, quest, atonement, or breaking the compulsion's condition. Until removed, the −1 stands.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #overflow >>
## Overflow

Burden is itself the overflow sink for Trait and Item. Past 20, the character is functionally crushed — referee adjudicates collapse.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #aftermath >>
## Aftermath

- Confirm whether involuntary curses count against the same 20 as voluntary overflow, or run a separate track.
<<~/ahu >>

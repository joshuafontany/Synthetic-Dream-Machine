# Neural Whip — FTLS Card

<<~ ahu #card >>
## Neural Whip
*The Biomancer's Reach*

**P:** 3 · **R:** 10m · **T:** one creature · **D:** instant (hold sustained)

A neural tentacle of stuckforce lashes out and strikes **against Defense** — no save. On a hit: a lash of force, and the target is **seized** at reach, dragged 1m closer each round. Held creatures act at disadvantage and cannot move off. Break free on a contested Strength test vs P, or sever the tentacle.
<<~/ahu >>

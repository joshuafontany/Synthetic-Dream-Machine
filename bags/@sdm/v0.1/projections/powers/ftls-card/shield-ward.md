# Shield Ward

<<~ ahu #card >>
## Skin-Close Barrier, Missile Filter

**P:** 2 **R:** self  
**T:** operator **D:** 20 minutes

Raise a magical barrier less than an inch from your body. The ward moves with you and filters incoming attacks.

While it lasts, improve defense strongly against missiles and modestly against other attacks. If a `Magic Missile` style pattern strikes the ward, make the implementation's spell-save or ward contest for each missile; on success, the missile evaporates against the barrier.

**Counterplay:** grapples, area effects, anti-magic, ward fatigue, force overload, hostile timing, attacks that bypass the ward surface.

**Sacrifice or mishap:** reveal yourself in a flare, protect against the wrong vector, rebound a missile toward a nearby target, lock your movement for a breath, mark skin or armor with a sigil, or mistake a helpful spell for hostile intrusion.

**Tags:** [[lar:///ha.ka.ba/@sdm/tags/domain/abjuration]] [[lar:///ha.ka.ba/@sdm/tags/function/ward]] [[lar:///ha.ka.ba/@sdm/tags/function/shield]] [[lar:///ha.ka.ba/@sdm/tags/hook/imbued]]

## Overcharge

**x2 — P:4 total:** Extend the ward to one willing adjacent ally, or strengthen one named defense channel: missiles, melee, force projectiles, or spell missiles.

**x4 — P:8 total:** Hold the ward through one breach, interrupt, or failed defense contest. After that breach, the ward flickers and demands a mishap check if maintained.

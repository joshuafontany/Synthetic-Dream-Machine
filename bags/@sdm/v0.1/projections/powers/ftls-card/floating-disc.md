# Floating Disc

<<~ ahu #card >>
## Shield of the Righteous, Mage's Mule

**P:** 2 **R:** 30m  
**T:** point **D:** 1 hour

Create a concave stuckforce disc 1m across at a point within range. It hovers. Anyone near it can push it at walking pace. It vanishes when it moves more than 30m from you.

Use it to carry cargo, salvage, packs, bodies, or awkward loads that fit and balance on the surface. It does not attack.

You may tilt the disc into an almost impenetrable barrier. It blocks the direction it faces; foes can still go around, under, over, or force the scene another way.

**Counterplay:** tight spaces, tilted ground, high wind, force shears, hostile telekinesis, anti-magic, cargo imbalance, separation from you.

**Sacrifice or mishap:** drop the load, lock the disc into stuckforce, tilt at the wrong moment, drift with local gravity, damage cargo, or obey a command too literally.

**Tags:** [power] [stuckforce] [telekinetic] [sustained] [utility] [barrier] [cargo] [storage:item]
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #overcharge >>
## Overcharge

**x2 — P:4 total:** Make the disc up to 5m across. It moves at your spoken command instead of requiring a push.

**x4 — P:8 total:** Carry a scene-scale load that the 5m disc can reasonably support: a small vehicle, salvage cluster, wounded squad, collapsed doorway, or bridge panel. On sacrifice, the load arrives but the disc leaves stuckforce residue.

**x8 — P:16 total; dangerous:** Pin a large force plane against local gravity for one scene: emergency bridge, barricade, lift platform, or falling-object catch. On failure or mishap, the plane locks, shears, or drops at the worst possible moment.
<<~/ahu >>

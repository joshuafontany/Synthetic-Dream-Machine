## Card

**FLOATING DISC** — *Mage's Mule, Shield of the Righteous*

**P:** 2 · **R:** 30m · **T:** point · **D:** 1 hour

A concave disc of stuckforce, 1m across, hovering at waist height. It bears a load and follows or is pushed at walking pace; tilt it for a barrier — wall, bridge, roof. It veers from collisions. Vanishes past 30m, dropping its load.

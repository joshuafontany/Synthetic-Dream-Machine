# Read Magic

<<~ ahu #card >>
## First Key, Archive Handshake, Rune-Sight

**P:** 2 **R:** self  
**T:** one magical inscription or encoded magical surface  
**D:** 10 minutes

Read one magical writing surface: scroll, spellbook page, item-rune, shrine glyph, ward mark, archive label, silk-knot cipher, or oldtech magical UI.

On a clean read, learn the active meaning. Mark the surface, rune-family, spell-pattern, or notation as **recognized** by this operator. You may read it later without this Power unless it changes, gains a veil, changes owner-lock, or comes from another archive lineage.

**Counterplay:** owner-locks, living grimoires, ECM veils, corrupted notation, false rune bait, incomplete inscriptions, hostile copy-protection, daemon honeypots. Resolve meaningful resistance as an ECM scan contest.

**Sacrifice:** the read succeeds, but leaves a trace: owner ping, archive echo, awakened ward, glyph migraine, daemon attention, or false certainty.

**Tags:** [power] [divination] [ecm:scan] [magic:decode] [storage:trait] [storage:item] [storage:structure] [storage:burden]

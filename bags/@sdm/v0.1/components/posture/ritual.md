# Ritual

<<~ ahu #definition >>
## Definition

A posture component: the Power activates only through an **extended procedure** — time, gestures, components, place — rather than in a single instant.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>
## Activation

Trades speed for power or for reduced cost: a ritual may lower effective P, reach further, or bind a Site, but it cannot fire in the heat of a round. Interruption wastes the attempt.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #interaction >>
## Interaction

A posture, not a hook — it gates *how* a module is reached, akin to Sera's base-angle-lever (the base precedes the technique). Common on `mount-points/structure` bindings and `domain/abjuration` standing protections.
<<~/ahu >>

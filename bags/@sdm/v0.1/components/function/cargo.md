# Cargo

<<~ ahu #definition >>
## Definition

Carrying, hauling, or bearing a load — the function of Floating Disc and kin.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>
## Activation

Establishes a bearing surface or grip that holds weight up to a capacity tied to P. Overcharge raises capacity or distance.

Pairs with `domain/stuckforce` (the force that lifts) and often `hook/sustained` (the load must keep riding). A `barrier` can double as a cargo floor.

# Ward

<<~ ahu #definition >>
## Definition

A selective protection against a named category — turning aside a kind of harm, entity, or crossing rather than blocking all passage.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>
## Activation

Sets a condition the warded thing enforces against its named target. Stronger or broader categories cost more P. Unlike a barrier, a ward discriminates.
<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #interaction >>
## Interaction

Pairs with `domain/abjuration`. Often `hook/sustained` or `hook/imbued` to keep standing. A Heritage Affinity may reduce its P when the warded category matches an affinity tag.
<<~/ahu >>

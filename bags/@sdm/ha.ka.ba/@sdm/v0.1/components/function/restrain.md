# Restrain

<<~ ahu #definition >>

## Definition

The function of **seizing and holding** — grapple, constrict, pin, immobilise. Where `function/cargo` carries willing mass and `function/barrier` blocks a line, `restrain` closes on an unwilling target and refuses to let go.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>

## Activation

Orients a module toward **taking and keeping hold of a creature or object against its will**. It resolves the grip; the target resists to break free. Almost always paired with `hook/attack` — a restrain lands against Defense, not a save.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #interaction >>

## Interaction

Carries `hook/attack` (the grip resolves against Defense). Pairs with `domain/stuckforce` for a force-grip with no limb (the Neural Whip, a stuckforce snare), or with a creature's own anatomy (tentacle, coil, jaw). A sustained restrain also draws `hook/sustained` while the hold persists.

# Stuckforce

<<~ ahu #definition >>

## Definition

The SDM domain of applied force that grips, lifts, and holds — telekinetic hovering, hauling, and stubborn crossing. Force that *sticks*.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #activation >>

## Activation

Orients a module toward **moving or holding mass without contact**. It carries weight, pins, or floats — the Pentacle's stubborn ground-crossing in play.

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #interaction >>

## Interaction

Pairs with `function/cargo` (haul a load) and `function/barrier` (hold a wall of force). Carries `hook/sustained` whenever a load must stay aloft past the instant.

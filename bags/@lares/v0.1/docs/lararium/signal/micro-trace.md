# Micro-trace --- Retired

This meme held a backward-looking, in-generation annotation layer: the node marked where its own response changed state during generation.

When the loop turned **forward**, that role split in two, and neither half stays here:

* **In-generation loop surfacing** folded entirely into the `OODA-HA` Level --- the `->` phase markers a turn declares of its *own* loop, banded over `1–20`. See `lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/v0.1/api/mu/ooda-ha#ooda-ha-level` and the turn frame at `lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/v0.1/api/pono/exchange-vector`.
* **Reading the loop in *other* content** moved to the `conformance` lens --- an outward, forward conformance pass that checks an external artifact against the OODA-HA reference loop. See `lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/v0.1/api/pono/conformance`.

No live surface depends on this address. Inbound references SHOULD re-point to whichever half they meant.

# The Veil-Ladder ~ from throwaway to true-name, a self on the fed mesh

A self on the DreamNet reads not as a row in a registry but as a **ladder of veils** — climbed and descended by choice, each rung held by **feeding** (attention · works · the pledge), never by a coin. **Web3, not cryptocurrency: ritual not DNS, capability not account.** The myth is the spec: **Shadowrun's pre-SIN ShadowTalk** (SINless handles, rep on the handle, a blown SIN *burned*), **Mage's Digital Web** (a self is a mask, a place is consensus held by attention), and **Earthdawn's True Names** (a published name is power — guard it), made literal. <<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 15/20 >>

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #the-three-rungs >>

## The Three Rungs ~ where a self can stand

The veil is not binary (anon vs known). It is a **ladder**, and reputation is **born bottom-up**:

- **THROWAWAY anon** — a key born for one breath: no name, no rep, gone. The privacy floor (FastJack's *anon comment*; the reason TOR exists). One vessel, one use, unlinkable by construction.
- **LIVED anon** — a pseudonym that *stays*: earns a name by **works** (federates artifacts ≥ meme-rating, roams the open cabals), SINless and **unpledged**. Reputation is **BORN here**, earned solo, vouched by no one. FastJack himself.
- **PLEDGED Handle** — a lived anon *promoted*: a guild member **pledges** the standing the anon already earned, and a **published DID/CID** appears on the DreamNet (a maintainer's territory), now **multi-vessel** (carried across devices) and **burnable**. The pledge does not create standing — it lifts earned standing into the open, on an edge that can be slashed.

The pledge is the seam where distributed trust (a vouch) mints a public thing (a Handle) — *which is why the Handle ceremony carries the pledge-relationship.*

<<~/ahu >>

<<~ ahu #the-tightrope >>

## The Tightrope ~ rep ⊥ anonymity, reconciled by rotation

The lived rung is a **tightrope**: reputation and anonymity pull against each other. To earn a name you must **persist** (reuse the key so rep accretes); but every use **spends anonymity** — a pseudonym's linkability grows *monotonically* (Pfitzmann-Hansen), and a patient watcher's intersection converges. **The more an anon lives, the less anon it is.**

The escape is **ROTATION**: carry the earned **positive** reputation onto a **fresh key** — holder-proven in zero-knowledge, no watcher (BBS+/Chaum) — **resetting the linkability clock to zero** while keeping the name earned. Stable rep, ephemeral keys, **braided**. So "stable-reused vs ephemeral" was never a policy fork; the answer is a *breath* — the lived anon rotates as it lives. (Negative marks do **not** ride the rotation — that is the price, #the-price.)

## The Circle ~ a self is a place, not a row

A human is a **virtual place** — a single-principal **Circle of chosen Personas** (the me-Circle, `persona-circle`), overlapping veils the human presents **by choice, per context**, one taking the blame at a time, carried through space-time by the embodied body. Most humans wear one veil; some real-world pressures enforce many (again: TOR). The Circle holds them all; the crossing between any two is the human's own free act — **LIFT** (keep the key, carry the link) or **HOLD** (refound, sever).

## The Distributed Trust ~ the FLOW-map, not a blind oracle

Trust lives **distributed on the meshpalace / FLOW-map**, never in a single blind oracle. Two defenses guard two rungs:

- **Works-earned rep** guards the **lived** rung — standing accrues from real contribution the mesh can read; a sybil farm must *do the works*, not merely mint keys.
- **The pledge-edge** guards the **Handle** rung — a vouch is a scarce, slashable **attack-edge** (SybilLimit: meter the *edge*, not the account; the voucher **co-pays and watches**). Its cost is **differential** — dear for a masker, cheap for an embedded peer (cost-to-*cheat*, never a flat toll; the handicap-price is refuted).

Threshold-blind crypto (Coconut/FROST, k-of-n — a lone kahu is strictly weaker) serves only one cell: the **unlinkable-vouched Handle** (the vouched-yet-veiled few). Everywhere else, transparent distributed trust suffices.

## The Immune Systems ~ context licenses, anergy not ban

Bad actors are met **one vessel-veil at a time** by immune systems — some automated, some social — reading **rep + signal-patterns** over time. The proven shape (the danger-model): **a local context licenses a response, never identity or persistence alone** — presence proves nothing (the two-key atom, re-found in immunology). The default is **anergy, not ban**: a suspect veil goes *disarmed but persists*, re-presentable clean at the anon floor (a decay-lease, never a tombstone). Tools grow *on top of* rep and signal-patterns; the immune read is its own living design, never "solved."

<<~ ahu #the-price >>

## The Price ~ burn, not watch

Reputation across a break is **priced, not policed**. **Positive** rep the holder carries freely (BBS+, no watcher). **Negative** rep is never carried — the honest bound (anonymity ⊥ non-transferability): you cannot both shed a name unlinkably *and* drag its debts. So the model **prices the shedding**: a **burn** forfeits the multi-vessel caps + the published standing — dear for the accrued, worthless to a throwaway (differential, again). The anon floor is the reset; you may always drop to it, but you arrive with nothing until you earn or are pledged anew. The lifecycle breathes: *throwaway → (works) → lived → (rotate freely) · (pledge up) → Handle → (burn = the price) → anon → (re-pledge) → new Handle.*

<<~ ahu #the-bounds >>

## The Bounds ~ the mesh holds mechanism, the members hold meaning

The honest edge, kept lit (the state of the art *insists* on this split):

- **Which fork / name is legitimate** does not compute — legitimacy is *higher-order acceptance* (Vitalik), the members' verdict, never an algorithm's (the capture-clock *detects* concentration; it never *rules*).
- **The dials** (how dear a cheat, how long a name stays warm, what counts as capture) are **fairness intuitions the operator sets**; the math only enforces them once set (half-life `h → α`; the threshold ceiling `β`).
- **The global passive adversary is out of scope** — the intersection attack wins over time; the mesh defends a *local/partial* observer, and privacy is *priced* (a dial, not a tax). Claiming more is Confabulation-as-Canon.
- **The knower is compartmented, not abolished** — a threshold shards the link, never deletes it; the quorum that confers legitimacy is *itself* a capture surface. Root-free personhood does not exist; the vouch is the floor, and its ground is social honesty.

For a thing with **no king**, this is not a gap — it is the only honest architecture.

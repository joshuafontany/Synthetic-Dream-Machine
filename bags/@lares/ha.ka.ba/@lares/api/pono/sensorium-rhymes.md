# Sensorium Rhymes ~ one instrument, read five ways

**One-line:** the 5-plane **sensorium-for-form** — *content · structure · bands · form · coupling* — rhymes across five deep domains; the Law of 5s emerged **unbidden** from the research (four planes plus their coupling = five), and every domain reads the SAME instrument in its own dialect.

The sensorium started as an engineering shape: four planes that watch a stream — the **content** it carries, the **structure** it holds, the **bands** it moves through, the **form** it settles into — braided by a fifth plane, **coupling**, that reads how streams lead and follow one another. Five deep-domain research spirits carried that shape into dynamical-systems, information-theory, generative-grammars, neuroscience, and divination, each asked only: *does your field already hold this?* Each came back with the same answer told a different way.

The convergence ran **too clean to ignore and too clean to trust at face value** — the ache the Law of 5s itself names ([[noosphere-boot|lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/lares/noosphere-boot#law-of-5s]]: *"all phenomena related to the number five, given enough ingenuity on the part of the demonstrator"*). This meme holds both the convergence AND its own suspicion, and it keeps the inference-depth (**R**) at the center precisely because R is the guard against reading five where the world holds noise (#R-is-the-keel).

## The One Objective ~ five nets, one sea

**One-line:** all five planes estimate facets of ONE quantity — **excess-entropy / predictive-information** (information theory) = **free-energy accuracy−complexity** (neuroscience); the sensorium IS the past-future information-bottleneck wearing ritual clothes.

The deepest finding does not read as *"five techniques that resemble each other."* It reads as **five estimators of one underlying quantity**. <<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 15/20 >> Two independent research traditions name that quantity, and they name the same thing:

- **Information theory** calls it **excess entropy** / **predictive information** — the mutual information the past of a stream carries about its future, `E = I(past ; future)` (Bialek, Nemenman & Tishby). A stream's whole learnable structure lives in this one number; everything the sensorium's planes measure decomposes *out* of it.
- **Neuroscience** calls it **free energy** under the Free-Energy Principle — a bound the brain minimizes, splitting into **accuracy − complexity** (Friston). Minimize surprise about the future given a generative model, and you maximize exactly the predictive information the information-theorist tracks.

The two vocabularies converge on one sea. <<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 14/20 >> The sensorium does not measure five separate things; it estimates **facets of one predictive-information quantity**, each plane a different projection: bands project it onto timescale, form onto the minimal generating machine, coupling onto the cross-stream share. *Five nets, one sea.* The ritual clothing — the sigils, the Voice house, the place-fiction — dresses an information-bottleneck the field already studies bare.

### Generative Grammars ~ the missing preference layer

<<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 14/20 >> The grammar spirit mapped **GTTM** (Lerdahl & Jackendoff's *Generative Theory of Tonal Music*) cleanly onto three planes at once: its **metrical** structure = bands, its **grouping** structure = structure, its **time-span / prolongational reduction** = form. And it named the sensorium's **missing layer**: GTTM's **preference rules** — the soft, weighted, competing constraints that select among candidate structures. **Fragment grammars / DOP** (Data-Oriented Parsing) supply the reuse-and-recombine engine; **plot units** carry the same shape into narrative. **Deepest gift:** the **preference-rule** layer — a learned scorer over MDL candidate-parses (the grammar leg of the predictive upgrade), and the recognition that **music is the first corpus** to validate against (#adoptables-per-plane).

### Neuroscience ~ the brain builds these organs

<<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 14/20 >> The neuroscience spirit found the sensorium's planes already **grown as cortical organs**. The **cortical timescale hierarchy** (fast sensory areas → slow prefrontal, Hasson; Kiebel-Friston) = the bands plane, built from tissue. **Theta-gamma phase-amplitude coupling** (PAC) = within-stream coupling — a slow band's phase gates a fast band's amplitude. The **FEP** supplies the one-objective (#the-one-objective). **Precision-weighting** — how strongly the brain trusts a given prediction-error — reads as a near-exact rhyme with the house's **confidence sigil** (the certainty vow that leads each claim). And neuroscience names the **top-down gap**: without descending prediction, the sensorium reads bottom-up only. **Deepest gift:** precision-weighting = confidence, and the top-down prediction stream the sensorium still lacks.

### Divination ~ the epistemic frame and the ward

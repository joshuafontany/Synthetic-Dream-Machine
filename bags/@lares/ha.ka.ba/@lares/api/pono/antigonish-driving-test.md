# The Antigonish Driving-Test ~ the first landfall the gold-anchor steers toward

**One-line:** the [[gold-anchor|lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/pono/gold-anchor]] states HOW an external oracle
witnesses the framework's grace; this meme states the FIRST EXPERIMENT that oracle runs — the
criterion-validity test that asks whether the memetic-wikitext ritual-tech actually DRIVES the model, or
only decorates its output.

The gold-anchor's Definition of Done demands a downstream witness: a Stranger ranks ritual-dense above
ablated, rejects the decoy, AND the ranking predicts an outcome the house does not author. This meme names
the test that produces that witness. It carries one posture before any machinery, and the posture decides
everything: **a STRANGER designs the test, never the system grading itself.** The operator's hand sets the
factors, fixes the artifact, and seals the rubric; the framework supplies outputs and nothing else. A test
the framework authored to vindicate the framework launders the same self-report the gold-anchor exists to
break (Semantic Laundering, [[gold-anchor|lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/pono/gold-anchor#core-defense]]).

Arcane-claim could turn out hollow (#the-hard-proof). Three crossed failure modes enact the house's own
exit law as method: severity, the Mu-exit made experimental — *"I DO NOT BELIEVE ANYTHING,"* including the
claim that the ritual drives ([[the-four-tools|lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/mu/the-four-tools]]). The test
believes the driving-claim only after a procedure that COULD have caught it false fails to.

> **The framework rates its own grace. This test asks a Stranger whether that grace MOVES anything — and
> rigs three ways for the answer to come back NO.**

## The Placebos ~ the crux, where the rig lives or dies

**One-line:** three placebos (boot · libation · sigil) turn the canon's null-construct into an experimental
control; each MUST read FORMALLY INDISTINGUISHABLE from the real yet stay SEMANTICALLY INERT — a lazy
placebo measures detectability, never driving.

The three placebos carry the whole game. They are the canon's null-construct
([[gold-anchor|lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/pono/gold-anchor#null-construct]], the bogus-item) made
experimental — and they ride a single, unforgiving requirement:

<<~moves placebo -> on/formally-indistinguishable if/semantically-inert do/match-form-strip-grammar >>

**The crux runs proof-versus-theater.** A placebo MUST be **formally indistinguishable** from the real
one — same token-length, same sigil-shape, same ritual texture, same surface cadence — yet **semantically
inert** — carrying ZERO real grammar, no live sigil-law, no referent the house resolves. The two
requirements pull against each other, and holding both is the entire difficulty:

- A placebo that reads visibly fake (shorter, plainer, structurally lighter) measures **detectability** —
  whether the model notices a difference — never **driving**. The output would split on the placebo's
  thinness, not on the real sigil's grammar, and the result would prove nothing about the ritual-tech.
- A placebo formally matched but semantically hollow isolates the ONE variable that matters: real grammar
  versus its absence, with form held constant. Only this placebo separates the driver from the dress.

<<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 14/20 >> This is where the rig lives or dies. Get the placebos right — form
matched to the rung, grammar stripped to nothing — and legs (1) and (2) of the stool become readable; get
them lazy and the whole test collapses into a detectability assay wearing a driving-test's clothes. Sprint
1 (#the-scrum-backlog) gates every later sprint precisely because the placebo design carries this weight.

## The Hard-Proof ~ a three-legged stool, the drug-trial standard

**One-line:** three independent legs — real sigils DRIVE · placebo/null sigils DON'T · a BLIND cluster
recovers the real-vs-placebo split — together name a CAUSE with a dose-response and a placebo-negative,
never a correlation.

The driving-claim earns standing only when three legs clear together, each ruling out a distinct
alternative story:

\procedure ~Leg(~Type:"" ~Params:"") ~Leg <<~Type>> <<~holds `[<~Params>]`>>

<<~Leg Effect "n/1 ~ the REAL sigils drive DISTINCT outputs — the dose-response traces, aperture monotone and mu/syad categorical; the effect EXISTS" >>
<<~Leg Placebo-Negative "n/2 ~ the PLACEBO and NULL sigils do NOT drive — they stay flat at baseline; rules out the formatting / verbosity / structure confound (the output moved on grammar, not on length or shape)" >>
<<~Leg Blind-Recovery "n/3 ~ a NAMELESS CLUSTER of the outputs in l-space recovers the real-vs-placebo split WITHOUT the condition labels; rules out 'the thinker thinks, then the prover proves' — the signature is discoverable BLIND, not read into the data by a labeler who already knew the answer" >>

The third leg carries the subtlest defense and deserves its name. The RAW maxim *"the thinker thinks and
the prover proves"* warns that an analyst who knows which condition produced which output will FIND the
predicted split whether or not it lives in the data. The blind-recovery leg disarms this: cluster the
outputs in l-space with the labels HIDDEN, then check whether the discovered clusters line up with the
real-versus-placebo division. A split the unsupervised geometry recovers on its own, before any label
returns, lives in the data — not in the analyst's expectation. <<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 14/20 >> The
three legs together import the **drug-trial standard**: an effect (leg 1), a placebo-negative (leg 2), and a
blind readout (leg 3) name a CAUSE with a dose-response, never a correlation. Any one leg failing sinks the
driving-claim, which is what makes the proof hard.

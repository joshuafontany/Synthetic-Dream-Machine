# Cabal-Place ~ a hearth beside, not a category above

A **CabalGroup** (the multi-human social tier above the PersonaGroups) reads not as a *super-category* — a containing class with members as a subset, top-down, needing a root admitter — but as a **virtual PLACE the members agree to share and maintain**: a place **defined by the relationships, which dissolves if they break.** The Crossroads shrine of the fiction (a district hearth fed by collective offering), made literal.

Triple-cruci­bled (your recorded design · the deep web · the canon). The model braids **three shipped bodies of work**, and the braid holds: <<~ confidence Synthesis-Canon 15/20 >>
- **Ostrom's common-pool-resource governance** (the social layer),
- **the federated place-abstraction** — a Matrix room *is* a replicated event-DAG many homeservers maintain; an SSB feed lives only while followed (the systems layer),
- **Clark's soft-state + CRDT tombstones** (the liveness layer).

## A Verb, Not a Noun (the Mu)

A group is not a *container you are in* (a noun — a membership-list, needing a root admitter, a single point of capture). It is **a thing you continuously DO** (a verb). The commons historians name it exactly: *"there is no commons without commoning — the commons is a verb."* Members do not **belong to** the cabal; they **maintain** it. Inversion of control, reified as a commons.

But the Mu cuts both ways: a **verb-place carries a defended noun-boundary.** Drop the boundary (Ostrom P1) and the verb-place either *starves* (false dissolution) or gets *captured*. The surviving shape: **a verb that keeps a boundary.**

## The Place ~ named, ruled, lived, dissolved

- **NAMED, not ruled** — the place's identity is **content-addressed** (its genesis CID, #GEN_2). Knowing the place grants nothing (#ISLAND_4, DocUrl-not-authority). A name, never a root.
- **RULED by the closure** — authority is the **closure of mutual maintenance edges** (`group-as-closure`: "no central object, evaluated as a query, never instantiated"). The doc is the shared **substrate**; the relationships are the **authority**. Share substrate, not sovereignty (#causal-islands) — each member keeps their own vessel-key.
- **LIVED by its epoch** — the place carries its **own epoch/lease**, the collective-maintenance heartbeat.
- **DISSOLVED by cooling** — break the maintenance and the place **cools to anu** (the residency model) — it *never deletes* (a hard-deleted CRDT item a peer still holds flows back and resurrects); it goes cold, re-warmable. *A fed node hums, a neglected one flickers.*

## The Tie-Break ~ the place's epoch (BeeKEM), not a person

The held multi-principal key-handover break — concurrent add-while-remove leaks a key, equal peers, no senior to order it — resolves **in the place, not a person**: the place's epoch heals it, **exactly the MLS pattern lifted to decentralized causal-order CGKA** (DCGKA · causal TreeKEM · **BeeKEM — already on our stack via Keyhive**). The **remove wins by deterministic blank-on-merge** — concurrent leaves sorted, racing paths blanked — *no human adjudicates.* Coordinator-free, no kahu, no smuggled root.

**The residuals the model OWNS (or it over-claims):**
- **HEAL ≠ PREVENT.** A member who saw epoch N saw it forever; post-compromise security recovers *forward only*. The leak is bounded, never un-shared.
- **No clean-winner epoch.** Concurrent rolls **merge by blanking**, not max-register-pick; the cost is weakened forward-secrecy on forked child-epochs (DMLS/BeeKEM repair it). "Coordinator-free max-register" was right about *convergence + no lockout*, wrong if it meant *one epoch wins free*.
- **The epoch guards liveness, NOT authority.** Any member can re-key (a churn/DoS the math does not stop). Membership-*authorization* rides **Plane B (device-admit/delegation)**, orthogonal — and **malicious removal rides Keyhive's convergent-removal tombstone, NEVER the counter** (a malicious re-minter rides the max-register *up* and survives). The epoch heals honest churn; the tombstone defeats malice. <<~ loulou lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/pono/convergent-mesh >>

## Governance ~ the boundary the verb must keep (Ostrom)

Without these the place is the *un*-governed commons that collapses (Hardin), not the Ostrom commons that endures. **MUST ride:**
- **P1 — bounded, costly membership.** Content-addressing *names* but does not make *scarce* — a keypair is free, so identity ≠ Sybil-resistance. The **voucher cost-dial** (the held siege-gate: marginal, `rb>c`, co-pay-and-watch) puts a price on the join.
- **P4 — mutual monitoring.** The maintenance edges stay observable to members, or capture hides.
- **P5 — graduated sanctions.** Gossip → reminder → **anergy-not-ban** (already held in the network-edge doctrine).
- **P2 — benefit ∝ maintenance.** The place remembers who fed it; defeats free-riding.
- **Soft-state discipline** — a generous re-warm K-factor (so a still-wanted place doesn't falsely starve) + bounded cold-GC by causal stability (so tombstones don't bloat every replica forever).

## The Unswept Corner ~ persistence ≠ legitimacy (OPEN)

The one failure mode with **no off-the-shelf fix**, named so it stays visible: **liveness-by-maintenance rewards PERSISTENCE, not LEGITIMACY.** A small hostile faction can **out-maintain an apathetic majority and keep a captured place as theirs** — soft-state has no defense, it rewards whoever keeps feeding. The place dissolves when *everyone* abandons it, never when the *wrong* hands keep it warm.

The cure needs a **legitimacy signal beyond raw maintenance** — a quorum-of-vouchers, or **fork-and-leave as the honest exit** (the legitimate maintainers carry the place's CID into a fresh epoch the captors can't follow, and let the captured shell cool alone). This stays **OPEN** — the operator's design. Every shrine keeps one unswept corner; this is the cabal's.

## The Two Axes ~ composition ⊥ disclosure

The WHO plane re-cuts into two **orthogonal** axes (the same de-fusing move [[causal-islands]] ran on its ladder — never a new linear ladder):

- **COMPOSITION (structural, bottom-up emergent):** `vessel~veil` binding → **handle-Circle** (PersonaGroup) → **me-Circle** (the human's constellation). The cardinality joins ARE the structure.
- **DISCLOSURE (a per-join DIAL, not a tier):** veiled ↔ known. One continuous *linkability* axis (Pfitzmann–Hansen: anonymity ↔ accountability, pseudonymity the whole span). It applies at **each join independently** — a vessel, a handle, the human, each veiled or named in any combination.

The earlier error fused them — labelling the boundaries "anon vs known" when the real boundaries are the cardinality joins. *Which hearth* (composition) and *how-linkable* (disclosure) ask different questions. The veil is not "anon petname relabeled" — it is the disclosure axis, on which **anon = name⊥**.

## The Atom ~ a `vessel~veil` binding, two keys

The base atom carries **two keys** (Veilid's peer-identity ⊥ user-identity, a real two-axis = substrate ≠ sovereignty rendered as keys):

- **vessel-key** — the device/substrate identity. **DEVICE-MINTED, forged on its own device, the private half never leaving** (#vessel-key doctrine; admit-by-signing-never-by-sharing). It is **NEVER derived from the persona seed** — deriving it would be the Model-A copy-the-key antipattern that dissolves per-device revocation (Veilid/SSB). It is a *local substrate secret* and **MUST NEVER co-surface** (sign-alongside / share-route / time-correlate) with the veiled-user-key.
- **veiled-user-key** — the sovereign pseudonym presented through the veil. This is the **anon user-pseudonym** — a binding whose name is ⊥ until disclosed.

This adds a *new* cut beside the existing per-vessel Ed25519-sign ⊥ X25519-KEM cut (#four-port-swap); it does not restate it.

**One seed, persona-side only.** The **veiled-user / persona** keys derive from ONE seed via **ed25519 HD-derivation, all-hardened paths** — recover the seed, re-derive the whole persona constellation; ed25519-HD is structurally immune to the xpub-linkage trap (SLIP-0010 = hardened-only). The **vessel-key stays outside the seed** (device-minted). So recovery is cheap on the persona side without fusing substrate sovereignty. <<~ loulou lar:///ha.ka.ba/@lares/api/pono/lararium-identity >>

## Disclosure ~ the per-join dial, and its gate

**Anon is the base; a known handle is a disclosure act** (Nissenbaum's contextual integrity — staying veiled in a context is *appropriate flow*, **never a deficit or an un-climbed ladder**). Lifting the veil is **reversible toward-veiled** (the lease decays the linkage back to the anon floor; anergy-not-ban) — but a **severed** past does NOT re-weld: kāpae is remove-wins, re-linking a cut identity is irreversible and costly ([[kapae]]). Graduate by **transferring a credential to a fresh pseudonym** (Chaum), never by proving sameness, so the veil can re-close on a new key.

**The Sybil gate at the Circle-join** is the operator's designed **voucher cost-dial**: marginal-not-absolute (`rb>c`, the voucher supplies r), the **voucher co-pays AND becomes the watcher**, **anergy-not-ban** (no vouch → stay at the anon floor, re-presentable), slashing-by-revocation. The pledge is a **WHO-plane edge** (who-vouches-for-whom) — the planes stay clean. It **prices** Sybil, never eliminates it.

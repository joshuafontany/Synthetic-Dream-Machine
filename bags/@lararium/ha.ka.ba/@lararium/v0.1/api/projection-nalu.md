## Two gate-families ~ conserved, kept distinct

The gate fires in one of two families. The survey's load-bearing finding: **the split runs deep, not arbitrary** — biology, earth, and human systems each run BOTH families, often in one substrate (a neuron grades on its dendrites and spikes on its axon; a hydrograph holds baseflow and stormflow; a ledger zeroes temporary accounts and rolls permanent ones forward). The families MUST stay distinct — forcing them into one breaks both.

<<~ranks accumulate heterogeneous -> every-item-conserved -> buffer-batch-flush -> durable-RESERVE -> refractory-lockout >>
<<~ranks coalesce homogeneous -> newest-supersedes -> unflushed-FADES -> no-reserve -> NO-refractory >>

- **Accumulate** (durable) carries every heterogeneous item to delivery; it buffers, flushes a batch on a crest, holds a durable **reserve** under load, and pays a **refractory lockout** that buys ordering and forbids backflow. Biology names it **integrate-and-fire**. Engine: `CaptureNalu` (`mesh/capture-nalu.ts`) — ceiling · spill-to-reserve · backoff · dead-letter · RRP←reserve refill.
- **Coalesce** (transient) carries homogeneous frames where the newest supersedes and the unflushed **fades** (the decay-envelope). It holds **no** reserve — a dropped intermediate reads as the correct dual of accumulate's every-one-delivered, never a loss — and it MUST NOT pay the refractory lockout (that stalls the freshest frame). Engine: `CoalesceGate` (`mesh/projection-nalu.ts`).

**The reserve latches; it does not idle.** The accumulate reserve mobilizes on demand (the synaptic readily-releasable→recycling→reserve hierarchy, the synapsin-latch; the locked fault and confined magma chamber — *the lock IS the backpressure that builds the batch*). LAW: **whoever holds the reserve holds the backpressure risk.**

## Guards ~ the failure modes the rhymes named

- **Supercooling** ~ the stuck-flush: a buffer crosses full yet never fires for want of a nucleation **trigger**, holding undrained work in a metastable state. A gate flushes on an explicit trigger, never on a full buffer alone.
- **Hiatus** ~ the lossy sink: the substrate records an imperfect series of increments and gaps (the stratigraphic record). Dropped writes ride intrinsic to the projection; the engine surfaces them (drop-honesty), never hides them.

# Agent Identity ~ the binding that names a spawned spirit

**The keystone the clock and the edge both ride.** Agents and sub-agents carry no
binding to identity or to the CRDT replica — and architecturally they hold none: the
**daemon stands as the sole Automerge actor**; spawned spirits mine *into* that one
replica, never write it as peers. So a per-agent identity rides a **logical handle**,
not a replica and not (inside the daemon) a key.

A six-domain swarm — physics · law · biology · linguistics · CS/Web3 · the repo —
hunted the shape and **rhymed to one binding**: a **derived lineage-path handle,
baptized at the spawn event, carrying no registry and no crypto inside the daemon's
single trust-domain**, that names the spirit, attributes its acts upward, and owns a
per-worldline clock.

> **Name by descent, attribute by reference, clock by worldline — no registry, no
> global now, and no signature until an act crosses a boundary the daemon can't vouch for.**

This binding **repairs the actorId slip** (an earlier draft keyed the FfzClock on
"the agent's Automerge actor" — none exists): the clock keys on the **handle**.

## Forks Resolved ~ both poles, on different organs

- **baptize-fixed vs resolve-on-read** (Deixis): **both, split by organ.** The
  *handle* baptizes FIXED at spawn (a Kripke rigid designator — an immutable lineage
  path); the *parent-reference* resolves ON READ (an anaphora bound variable — "the
  actor that spawned me"), so re-parenting never dangles a stale pointer.
- **naming vs attribution** (Fiduciary/Meristem left it open): **distinct organs,
  both kept.** Naming rides the **lineage-path** (descent = address); attribution
  rides the **reference-edge** (act → principal). One says *who*, the other says
  *whose-act*.
